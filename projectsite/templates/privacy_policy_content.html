Introduction

Republic Act No. 10173 - Data Privacy Act of 2012

Real's Food Products is committed to protecting your personal data in full compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 and its Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information in accordance with Philippine data protection laws.

Welcome to Real's Food Products Inventory & Sales Management System. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our system.

By accessing or using our system, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use our system.

Information We Collect

1. Personal Information

We collect the following personal information when you register:

  • Account Information: Username, email address, first name, last name, and password (encrypted)
  • Profile Information: User role (Administrator or Staff) and account status

2. Automatically Collected Information

When you use our system, we automatically collect:

  • Login Activity: Login timestamps, IP addresses, device information, browser type, and operating system
  • Device Fingerprints: Unique identifiers for trusted device management
  • Session Data: Session duration and actions performed

3. Business Data

As part of the inventory management system, we process:

  • Product information, inventory records, and sales data
  • Transaction history and financial records
  • Reports and analytics data

How We Use Your Information

Purpose of Data Processing

We collect and process your personal data for the following specific, legitimate, and lawful purposes:

  • Account Management: Create and manage user accounts, authenticate users, and maintain account security
  • System Operations: Provide access to inventory management features and process transactions
  • Security: Detect and prevent unauthorized access, monitor suspicious activities, and fraud prevention
  • Communication: Send account notifications, security alerts, password reset emails, and system updates
  • Compliance: Maintain audit trails and comply with legal, tax, and regulatory obligations
  • Business Analytics: Generate reports and analytics for business decision-making

Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data based on the following legal grounds under RA 10173:

  • Consent: Your explicit consent when creating an account and using the system
  • Legitimate Interest: Business operations, security purposes, and fraud prevention
  • Legal Obligation: Compliance with Philippine tax laws, labor regulations, and business requirements
  • Contractual Necessity: Performance of employment contracts or service agreements with Real's Food Products

Purpose Limitation

We will only process your personal data for the purposes stated above. We will not use your data for purposes incompatible with these stated purposes without obtaining your prior consent.

Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your information:

Security Measures Include:
  • Encryption: All passwords are encrypted using industry-standard hashing algorithms
  • Secure Connections: Data transmission is protected using HTTPS/SSL encryption
  • Access Controls: Role-based access control limits data access based on user roles
  • Two-Factor Authentication: Optional 2FA adds an extra layer of security
  • Trusted Device Management: Device fingerprinting and OTP verification for new devices
  • Session Security: Automatic session expiration and secure session management
  • Regular Backups: Automated database backups to prevent data loss

Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy and to comply with legal obligations:

  • Active Accounts: Personal data is retained for the duration of your employment or authorization with Real's Food Products
  • Inactive Accounts: Personal information is anonymized after 30 days of account deactivation
  • Deleted Accounts: Soft-deleted accounts are anonymized within 90 days of deletion request
  • Transaction Records: Business and financial records are retained for 5 years to comply with tax and audit requirements under Philippine law
  • System Logs: Activity logs and security logs are retained for 1 year for security monitoring and troubleshooting
  • Backup Data: Backup copies are retained for 90 days and then permanently deleted

After the retention period expires, personal data will be securely deleted or anonymized in accordance with data protection standards.

Your Data Subject Rights (Under RA 10173)

As a data subject under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you have the following rights:
  • Right to be Informed: Be informed whether your personal data is being processed, including the purposes and extent of processing
  • Right to Access: Request access to and obtain a copy of your personal data stored in our system
  • Right to Object: Object to the processing of your personal data for certain purposes, including direct marketing and automated decision-making
  • Right to Erasure or Blocking: Request suspension, withdrawal, or deletion of your personal data from our systems
  • Right to Rectification: Correct, update, or dispute inaccurate or incomplete personal information
  • Right to Data Portability: Obtain your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format
  • Right to File a Complaint: Lodge complaints with the National Privacy Commission regarding data privacy violations
  • Right to Damages: Be indemnified for any damages sustained due to inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, false, or unlawfully obtained personal data

How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, you may:

  • Contact our Data Protection Officer at inventcaps@gmail.com
  • Submit a written request to your system administrator
  • Access your profile settings to update certain information directly

We will respond to your request within fifteen (15) days from receipt. If we need more time, we will inform you of the reasons for the delay.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We respect your privacy and do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Within Organization: Authorized administrators and staff members with appropriate role-based access permissions
  • Service Providers: Trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating the system (e.g., email service providers, hosting services) under strict confidentiality agreements
  • Legal Compliance: When required by law, court order, subpoena, or government regulation
  • Protection: To protect the rights, property, or safety of Real's Food Products, our users, or the public
  • Business Transfers: In connection with any merger, sale of company assets, or acquisition (with prior notice to affected users)

All third parties with whom we share data are required to maintain the confidentiality and security of your personal information in compliance with RA 10173.

Data Breach Notification

In the Event of a Personal Data Breach:

In accordance with RA 10173 and NPC Circular 16-03, if we discover a personal data breach that may pose a real risk to your rights and freedoms, we will:

  • Notify the National Privacy Commission within seventy-two (72) hours from knowledge of the breach
  • Notify affected data subjects without undue delay via email or system notification
  • Describe the nature of the breach, including the categories and approximate number of affected data subjects
  • Communicate the potential consequences of the breach and the likely risks
  • Outline the measures taken to address the breach and mitigate its possible adverse effects
  • Provide recommendations for affected users to protect themselves from potential harm

We maintain an incident response plan and conduct regular security assessments to minimize the risk of data breaches.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience and maintain system security:

  • Session Cookies: Essential for system functionality and user authentication (cannot be disabled)
  • Security Cookies: CSRF tokens to protect against cross-site request forgery attacks
  • Remember Me: Optional persistent cookies to keep you logged in across sessions
  • Device Fingerprinting: To identify trusted devices and enhance security through device recognition

These cookies expire when you close your browser or log out (session cookies) or after a specified period (persistent cookies). We do not use cookies to track your activity outside of this system.

Children's Privacy

This system is not intended for use by individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you are under 18 years old, please do not use this system or provide any personal information.

If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a minor without proper parental consent, we will take steps to delete such information immediately.
