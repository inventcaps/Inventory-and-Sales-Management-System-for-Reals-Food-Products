Introduction

Welcome to Real's Food Products Inventory & Sales Management System. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our system.

By accessing or using our system, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

1. Personal Information

We collect the following personal information when you register:

  • Account Information: Username, email address, first name, last name, and password (encrypted)
  • Profile Information: User role (Administrator or Staff) and account status

2. Automatically Collected Information

When you use our system, we automatically collect:

  • Login Activity: Login timestamps, IP addresses, device information, browser type, and operating system
  • Device Fingerprints: Unique identifiers for trusted device management
  • Session Data: Session duration and actions performed

3. Business Data

As part of the inventory management system, we process:

  • Product information, inventory records, and sales data
  • Transaction history and financial records
  • Reports and analytics data

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:

  • Account Management: Create and manage user accounts, authenticate users, and maintain account security
  • System Operations: Provide access to inventory management features and process transactions
  • Security: Detect and prevent unauthorized access, monitor suspicious activities
  • Communication: Send account notifications, security alerts, and password reset emails
  • Compliance: Maintain audit trails and comply with legal obligations

Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your information:

Security Measures Include:
  • Encryption: All passwords are encrypted using industry-standard hashing algorithms
  • Secure Connections: Data transmission is protected using HTTPS/SSL encryption
  • Access Controls: Role-based access control limits data access based on user roles
  • Two-Factor Authentication: Optional 2FA adds an extra layer of security
  • Trusted Device Management: Device fingerprinting and OTP verification for new devices
  • Session Security: Automatic session expiration and secure session management
  • Regular Backups: Automated database backups to prevent data loss

Data Retention

We retain your information as follows:

  • Active Accounts: Data is retained while your account is active
  • Inactive Accounts: Deactivated accounts are anonymized but retained for audit purposes
  • Deleted Accounts: Soft-deleted accounts are anonymized and retained for 90 days
  • Audit Logs: System logs are retained for compliance and security purposes

Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request access to your personal data stored in our system
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate personal information through your profile settings
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Data Portability: Request a copy of your data in a structured format

To exercise these rights, please contact your system administrator.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • Within Organization: Authorized administrators and staff members with appropriate access levels
  • Service Providers: Third-party services that help us operate the system (e.g., email service providers)
  • Legal Compliance: When required by law, court order, or government regulation
  • Protection: To protect the rights, property, or safety of Real's Food Products, our users, or others

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies:

  • Session Cookies: Essential for system functionality and user authentication
  • Security Cookies: CSRF tokens to protect against attacks
  • Remember Me: Optional persistent cookies to keep you logged in
  • Device Fingerprinting: To identify trusted devices and enhance security

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the updated policy and updating the "Last Updated" date.
